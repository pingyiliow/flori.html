What changes

  • One bar like Orders: the three views on the left, the dates on the right. The dates (Tomorrow / Next 3 days / Next 7 days / From–To) apply to all three views, so the day buttons and the boxed date buttons go.
  • A tally like Orders: flowers to buy, orders counted, orders that need a recipe.
  • Flower list splits into To buy and In stock. To buy is the big number; covered flowers say "Enough". Units sit under the number, so nothing wraps.
  • No grey squares: a photo shows only when the flower has one in Stock or Shopify.
  • Each flower says how many orders use it; tap to see them. "Copy to-buy list" moves to the top, as an outlined button.
  • By day uses the Orders row: time on the left, customer and order number, then the flowers one per line with the quantity first, easy to count off while making.
