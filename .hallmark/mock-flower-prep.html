"Add recipe" opens the Flower Recipe. Once a product has a standard recipe, its next orders fill in by themselves.

Why this layout

  1. Home stays short. Three numbers and one tap, not a long list on the dashboard.
  2. Flower list adds the flowers up for the dates chosen (presets, or pick dates), and compares with what is in Stock: Need, Stock, To buy. Copy sends the "to buy" list to the supplier on WhatsApp. Each flower opens to show which orders and days use it.
  3. By day is the florist's list: one day at a time, only orders that have a recipe, each opens the order.
  4. Needs recipe keeps the incomplete orders apart, with a direct Add recipe button, so they never clutter the other two.
  5. "Stock" uses the flowers already in Stock (when a recipe row is linked to a stock item). Flowers not in Stock show "–" and count fully as to buy.
